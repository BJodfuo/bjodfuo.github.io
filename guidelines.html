Community Guidelines

Rules and expectations for our Discord server

"PUNISHMENTS ARE FINAL DECISION OF AN ACTION. EVERY RULE LISTED OR UNLISTED MAY RESULT IN CUSTOM PUNISHMENTS."

Enforcement Overview

The badge shown on each rule indicates the minimum action taken upon a first-time offense. Repeated or severe infractions escalate higher.

None — Notice or reminder with no recorded penalty.
Warning — Verbal notice or official recorded warning.
Timeout / Mute — Temporary communication restriction.
Kick — Immediate server removal; able to rejoin.
Temporary Ban — Time-based removal before returning is allowed.
Permanent Ban — Permanent ban and automatic blacklist.
