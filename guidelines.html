Community Guidelines

Rules and expectations for our Discord server

"Respect the space, respect the members."

1
Follow Discord ToS

Adhere strictly to Discord Terms of Service and Community Guidelines at all times.

Punishment: Immediate Permanent Ban
2
Be Respectful

No harassment, hate speech, toxicity, excessive trolling, or personal attacks directed at any member or staff.

Punishment: Formal Warning / Mute / Escalated Ban
3
No Spamming

Avoid text wall spam, repetitive pings, emoji spam, or flooding chat channels with unnecessary media.

Punishment: Message Deletion / Temporary Timeout
4
Keep Content SFW

Explicit, sexually suggestive, gory, or NSFW content/links are strictly forbidden.

Punishment: Content Removal & Permanent Ban
5
No Unauthorized Advertising

Do not promote self-projects, external Discord servers, or commercial products without explicit staff approval.

Punishment: Link Deletion & Temporary Mute
6
Use Channels Correctly

Post discussions, bot commands, media, and feedback in their respective dedicated channels.

Punishment: Verbal Warning & Message Deletion
7
Follow Staff Directions

Moderator decisions are binding. If you have an issue with a moderation action, contact staff via DMs or support tickets respectfully.

Punishment: Timeout / Server Kick
Enforcement Overview

Moderators reserve the right to escalate punishments based on severity or repeated infractions:

Warning — Issued for minor or first-time offenses.
Timeout / Mute — Temporary restriction for spam, toxicity, or self-promo.
Kick — Removal from the server for repeat infractions.
Permanent Ban — Permanent removal for NSFW, severe harassment, or ToS violations.
